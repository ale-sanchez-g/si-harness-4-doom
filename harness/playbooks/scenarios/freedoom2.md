- [MAP02] Right at the start, the red keycard is NOT straight ahead: turn toward your right (roughly 40 degrees off your starting facing) and head that way for about 300-350 units. It sits in a small raised alcove past a shallow water/slime pit, tucked out of the direct sightline - walk into the pit and up onto the alcove to reach it; get it before exploring elsewhere.
- [MAP02] Once you have the red key, find the building in the southeast corner of the map to access it. Inside, you will find a lift against the east wall that you must ride down to the bottom and then back up to the top. At the top, look to the northwest to find the yellow keycard on a stair-stepped structure against the north wall.
